# Joseph Bethel | Data Analyst

## 🔍 What I Do for Businesses

Most businesses are sitting on data they cannot use, They have transaction records, customer information, sales figures, and operational data — but no clear picture of what it means or what to do about it.

I bridge that gap.

I work with businesses to turn raw, unstructured data into clear dashboards, actionable insights, and data-driven recommendations that support better decisions across banking, fraud detection, healthcare, retail, and customer analytics.

---

## 💼 How I Can Help Your Business

**If your business is losing customers** — I analyze churn patterns, identify high-risk segments, and deliver targeted retention strategies backed by data.

**If your business is exposed to fraud** — I build fraud detection dashboards that identify suspicious transaction patterns, flag high-risk activity, and support financial crime prevention.

**If your business has credit or lending risk** — I evaluate customer financial behavior, segment by risk level, and deliver insights that protect your lending portfolio.

**If your business needs clearer reporting** — I build interactive Power BI and Excel dashboards with KPI tracking, slicers, and drill-down visuals your team can actually use.
